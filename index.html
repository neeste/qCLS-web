Instructions

  • Listen to the stimulus and select the colored bar that best matches how loud you thought the sound was.
  • Use the bars that have labels and the bars in between that do not have labels.
  • Make each choice based on that sound alone and try not to let the sound you heard before influence your choice.
  • Remember, you are judging the loudness of the sound, not the pitch.

qCLS Control

Nothing is presented above this level. Lower it for a listener with reduced tolerance. It is also lowered automatically, by 5 dB, each time "Too Loud" is reported, and never raised during a run.
Leave any frequency blank if it was not measured. Blanks fall back to the population distribution for that band rather than to a guess. Check the units: the prior was fitted against dB SPL, so mislabeled dB HL shifts the low frequencies.
