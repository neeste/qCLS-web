Instructions

  • Listen to the stimulus and select the colored bar that best matches how loud you thought the sound was.
  • Use the bars that have labels and the bars in between that do not have labels.
  • Make each choice based on that sound alone and try not to let the sound you heard before influence your choice.
  • You may hear the same sound more than once, so it is okay to make the same choice, such as choosing "Loud" twice in a row.
  • Remember, you are judging the loudness of the sound, not the pitch.
  • There are no right or wrong answers. What matters is how you perceived the sound.
